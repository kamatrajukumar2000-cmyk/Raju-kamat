CVD MACHINE SYSTEM

Heating & Temperature System

Temperature control is machine-specific and may use heaters, sensors and optical measurement. This page organizes the main service areas.

Heater

Provides controlled heating of the relevant process or substrate area where fitted.

Heater Power Supply

Delivers controlled electrical power to the heating element.

Temperature Controller

Receives measurement input and manages the configured control loop.

Thermocouple

Contact temperature measurement where the machine uses thermocouples.

Pyrometer

Non-contact temperature measurement where an optical sensor is installed.

Temperature Display

Displays process temperature and relevant alarms through the control system.

Thermal Insulation

Helps manage heat transfer and temperature stability according to machine design.

Temperature Interlock

Protective logic can stop or limit operation when configured temperature conditions are exceeded.

First Checks

  • Compare the sensor reading with the machine's normal record.
  • Check for sensor alarms or disconnected measurement signals.
  • Review heater status and cooling status.
  • Record temperature before changing process settings.
  • Allow hot components to cool according to the manufacturer's procedure before access.
Safety: Process components can remain hot after shutdown. Use the manufacturer's cooldown and isolation procedure and trained personnel.
