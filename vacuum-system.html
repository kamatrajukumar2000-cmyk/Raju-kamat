CVD MACHINE SYSTEM

Vacuum & Chamber System

The vacuum system creates and maintains the required chamber environment. Use this page as a service reference for components, observations and first checks.

Vacuum Chamber

Main process enclosure containing the plasma and substrate area.

Vacuum Pump

Provides pump-down and maintains the required vacuum condition.

Vacuum Valves

Isolation, venting and process flow control components.

Throttle / Pressure Control

Used where fitted to regulate chamber pressure.

Vacuum Gauges & Sensors

Measure and report chamber pressure for control and diagnosis.

Quartz Window / Viewport

Provides viewing or coupling interface depending on machine design.

Seals & O-rings

Maintain chamber sealing at doors, flanges and service connections.

Exhaust Line

Connects the chamber and pumping system to the exhaust path.

First Checks

  • Review pump-down time and pressure trend.
  • Check valve positions and visible alarm states.
  • Inspect accessible seals, flanges and connections.
  • Compare pressure readings with the machine's normal operating record.
  • Do not open or service a pressurized/vacuum system without the manufacturer's procedure.
Safety: Vacuum chambers, pumps and associated equipment can present mechanical, electrical and process hazards. Follow the machine manufacturer's service procedure and trained-personnel requirements.
