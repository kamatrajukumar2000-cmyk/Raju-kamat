CVD MACHINE SYSTEM

Plasma System

Plasma generation and monitoring depend on the machine's chamber, gas, pressure and power architecture. This page organizes the major service areas.

Plasma Chamber

Process enclosure in which the plasma is generated and maintained.

Plasma Zone

The active region whose position and stability influence process conditions.

Ignition System

Machine-specific control and conditions used to establish plasma.

Plasma Monitoring

Visual, sensor or software monitoring depending on machine design.

Viewing / Optical Interface

Window or optical monitoring hardware where fitted.

Pressure & Flow Inputs

Inputs used by the control system to maintain process conditions.

Power Inputs

Microwave/RF power and related measurements feed the process-control loop.

Interlock Interface

Safety conditions can prevent or stop plasma operation when required.

First Checks

  • Record pressure, gas-flow and power readings before changing settings.
  • Check cooling and safety-interlock status.
  • Review recent alarms or changes in machine condition.
  • Observe plasma only through the machine's approved viewing method.
  • Do not bypass safety interlocks or operate outside the manufacturer's procedure.
Safety: Plasma equipment may involve high voltage, microwave/RF energy, hot surfaces and process gases. Inspection and service should be performed only by trained personnel.
