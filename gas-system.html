CVD MACHINE SYSTEM

Gas Supply & MFC System

Gas delivery, regulation, filtration and controlled flow are key parts of a stable CVD process. Exact gas architecture depends on the machine.

Gas Sources

Process-gas cylinders or other approved gas sources used by the machine.

Regulators

Reduce and control source pressure before gas enters the distribution system.

Gas Manifold

Routes individual gas supplies toward the machine's control and mixing path.

Mass Flow Controllers

Measure and control gas flow according to the machine control system.

Filters & Purification

Help control particles and contamination where specified by the system design.

Valves

Shut-off, check, solenoid and pneumatic valves may be used depending on architecture.

Pressure Sensors

Monitor gas pressure at relevant points in the delivery system.

Tubing & Fittings

Connect regulators, manifolds, MFCs and chamber gas inlets.

First Checks

  • Check machine alarms and gas-pressure indications.
  • Compare commanded and reported MFC flow.
  • Check approved supply pressure and valve status.
  • Look for abnormal flow, leaks or contamination indicators.
  • Use only the gas type, pressure and procedure specified for the machine.
Safety: Pressurized gases can be hazardous. Gas selection, connections, regulators and leak checks must follow the machine and gas-supplier safety procedures. Never bypass gas safety systems.
