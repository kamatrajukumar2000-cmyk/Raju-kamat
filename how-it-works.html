How Teacher–Student Consulting Works

Our goal is to make the connection process clear before any tuition starts.

1

Parent submits a requirement

Class, subject, mode, location, timing and special requirements are shared.

2

Teacher profile is reviewed

Teacher information is collected and reviewed for matching. Registration itself is not a guarantee of a student.

3

Suitable options are discussed

We use class, subject, location and teaching mode to identify potentially suitable options.

4

Parent and teacher confirm details

Availability, fees, timing, location, syllabus and teaching expectations are confirmed before starting.

5

Tuition begins by mutual agreement

The final teaching arrangement is between the parent/student and teacher. Guardian involvement is expected for minor students.
