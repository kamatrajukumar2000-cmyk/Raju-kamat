Safety, FAQ & Clarity

Important: We are a teacher–student consulting and connection platform. Registration or a request does not guarantee a job, student, teacher, fee, availability or placement.

Does teacher registration guarantee a student?

No. Registration only creates a teacher enquiry/profile request. Suitable student connections depend on actual requirements and availability.

Does a parent request guarantee a teacher?

No. A teacher must be available and suitable requirements must be mutually confirmed.

Who confirms the fee?

The parent/student and teacher should mutually confirm the fee and payment terms before classes begin.

Who confirms timing and location?

Both sides should confirm the exact schedule, location and teaching mode before starting.

Are teacher qualifications automatically verified?

Teacher information should be reviewed/verified before presenting it as confirmed. Parents should also ask for appropriate qualification or experience evidence before final selection.

Can minor students use the service alone?

Parents or guardians should be involved in teacher selection, communication and final tuition arrangements for minor students.

Will teacher phone numbers be publicly displayed?

No. We should avoid publishing personal contact details publicly. Initial communication can be coordinated through the platform/WhatsApp.

Who is responsible for the final tuition arrangement?

The final teaching arrangement, fees, schedule and ongoing tuition relationship are between the parent/student and teacher after mutual confirmation.
